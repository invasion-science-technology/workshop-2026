Participant reimbursements

Submit a reimbursement claim

Participants can submit reimbursement details and upload receipt images until June 30, 2026, 11:59 pm PT.

Claim details

Upload one or more receipt images. Accepted file types depend on your device, but photos such as JPG, PNG, HEIC, or WebP are preferred.

Payment details for reimbursement

Provide the payment details needed to reimburse you.

For US accounts, the local account ID is usually the routing number plus account number. For SEPA/EU accounts, use IBAN.

Select all relevant receipt images (PNG, JPG, WebP, HEIC, or HEIF) before submitting.

Deadline: June 30, 2026, end of day Pacific Time.

Important: Submitting a claim does not guarantee reimbursement. The organising committee may request additional documentation before processing.
